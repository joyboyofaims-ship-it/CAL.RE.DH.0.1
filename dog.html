📘 App guide: how to use this simulator (tap to open)

What this app is

Simulates an anaesthetised dog on a chart recorder (Ex. No. 10, effect of drugs on dog blood pressure, MBBS practical). Give drugs or apply procedures and watch SBP/DBP/MAP and HR respond live. Reproduces the 20-row observation table and the Q1 to Q8 answer key. Baseline: HR 150 bpm, BP 120/75 mmHg (MAP 90).

How to do the practical

  • Rows 1 to 15: New animal, pick drug at Standard dose, inject, wait until the trend returns to baseline, read Peak responses.
  • Rows 16 to 20: pick the row; the blocker is given, then the challenge drug automatically after 40 s. The table measures the second drug from the state just before it.
  • Own sequences: any order, e.g. Epi, NE, phentolamine, NE, Epi for the Q5 chart. Blockers stay on until you tap Washout blockers or New animal.
  • The dog can die: MAP below 40 for 15 s, HR below 40 for 10 s, MAP above 180 for 10 s, HR above 260 for 10 s. Ephedrine 200 is near the upper limit; High (2x) ephedrine kills the dog.

Phentolamine behaviour

Alpha blockade lasts until Washout or New animal (99% block at standard dose). The BP fall and reflex HR rise are temporary and fade with a time constant of about 40 s.

Where the record and textbooks disagree

The app follows your record in every case.

  • Carotid occlusion: record says carotid body chemoreceptors; standard mechanism is unloading of carotid sinus baroreceptors. The answer text gives both.
  • Central vagus: record shows a rise; many textbooks describe a reflex fall.
  • Adrenaline 0.1 mcg/kg: record gives no change; many books show a small fall in BP (beta-2).
  • Doses: record table says adrenaline 3 and noradrenaline 3; question text and charts say 6 (also adrenaline 2). Standard dose here is 3; High gives 6.
  • Row 16 absolute HR: record starts at 160, app at about 200 to 219. Net effect (+20 / +80) matches.
  • Washout: the Washout blockers button clears all blockers at once. This is a simplification, because real blockers wear off slowly.

Limits

  • Time courses, partial blocker doses, ACh 10/100/300 after atropine, and adrenaline at 0.3 to 2 and above 3 mcg/kg are assumptions, not recorded values.
  • Written answers exist for Q1 to Q8 only (none for histamine, mepyramine, cimetidine).
  • Question order and wording follow your answer key; page references were removed because they could not be verified.
  • Study aid only. For doses and drug content use KD Tripathi and the official Ex-Pharm manual.
